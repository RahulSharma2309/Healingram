# Flow — Discovery (homepage, header, footer)

**Who:** a person who is not sure which retreat to pick, arriving on Healingram for the first time.

**What they want:** start from a need, not from hotel dates.

## Happy path

1. They land on `/`. The header is already there. Explore Retreats is a single click to all retreats — no menu hiding behind it.
2. They read “Find retreats, practices and people that help you return to yourself.”
3. They choose a need (for example Stress & Burnout) and press Find Retreats. They arrive on results already filtered to that need, and they only see locations that actually have a matching retreat.
4. Or they are not sure — they use Find My Match (from the hero or footer, not the header).
5. Or they pick Karnataka or Kerala from the two destination cards.
6. If they still hesitate, “Talk to a Healingram Expert” takes them to the concierge form. We remember they came from the homepage destination area.

## What they will not see

Featured lists, star ratings, guest favourite, Goa/Rishikesh, a Why Healingram icon-card section, admin or vendor links in the public footer, Find My Match in the header.

## Recovery

- Need list fails to load: hero stays, Retry, plus Explore all retreats and Talk to an Expert.
- Find Retreats with nothing selected: “Choose what you are looking for first.”
- Session lookup fails: header looks logged out; we only mention the error if they try an account action.
