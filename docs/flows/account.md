# Flow — Account, wishlist, My Trips

**Who:** returning guests.

## Wishlist

They may heart a retreat while logged out (kept on this device). To keep it across devices they sign in. We ask before merging, and we do not duplicate the same retreat.

## My Requests and My Trips

- Requests stay reachable by a secure link or a signed-in account.
- Trips group into payment pending, upcoming, completed, cancelled/refund.
- If something fails we Retry. We never show another person’s trips.

## Header

Logged out: Log in. Logged in: My Trips and an account menu (wishlist, profile, support, partners, log out).
