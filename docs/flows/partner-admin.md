# Flow — Partner and admin operations

**Who:** retreat staff, and Healingram ops.

## Partner

1. They open the pending queue: who asked, which programme, dates, guests, shown price, how old the request is, whether they have opened it.
2. They either confirm the exact stay (final amount, taxes, room, inclusions), suggest a different stay, or mark unavailable.
3. We record when they first viewed and when they answered. We may highlight aging. We do not promise the guest a service-level time we cannot keep.

## Admin

1. They see the same pipeline with customer and retreat columns.
2. They may move a request only through allowed steps, confirm a final price, leave an **internal** note, resend a notification, or recreate payment-ready.
3. Every manual price or status change is attributed to them.
4. They cannot click “paid”. Only the payment provider’s verified webhook can.
