# Find my match

**Who:** a guest who is not sure which retreat fits.

## What they want

A short questionnaire that suggests **published** stays, with calm reasons — not a medical diagnosis and not a “#1 ranked” list.

## What they do

1. Open `/questionnaire` (Find My Match is **not** a header dropdown).
2. Answer the questions (time, practice interest, and similar).
3. See a list of matching retreat slugs and short reasons (“yoga on offer”, “fits your time”).
4. Tap a match and land on that listing.

## If it fails

Empty or broken answers stay on the page with a clear error. We do not invent a retreat to fill the screen.
