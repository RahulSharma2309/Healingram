# Browse catalog

**Who:** anyone (login not required).

## What they want

See real published stays, pick a need or a place, open a listing that is honest about price.

## What they do

1. Open the homepage. Need chips and places come from what is **published**, not from a hard-coded map of India.
2. Open **Explore retreats** (`/retreats`). They see seeded published stays (today: 14).
3. Filter by need, state, or city if those filters have inventory behind them.
4. Open a listing (example: Shathayu). They see name, locality, state, programmes, and a price only if the server says it is verified. Otherwise they see “on request” / after programme selection — never a made-up rupee amount.
5. If they guess a fake URL (`/retreats/not-a-real-place`), the stay is not invented. They get a missing page.

## What we will not show

Ratings, review counts, “guest favourite”, Featured rows, Goa/Rishikesh OTA cards that are not in the catalog.
