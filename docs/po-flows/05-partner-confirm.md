# Partner confirm

**Who:** the retreat partner (`partner@local.test` locally). Admin may also confirm.

## What they want

To say yes (with a final rupee amount), suggest other dates, or say those dates are not possible.

## What they do

1. Log in and land on `/vendor`.
2. See **pending** requests for retreats they are linked to — from the **server**, not only from their browser memory.
3. **Confirm** with a final payable amount in INR. The guest’s request becomes payment-ready.
4. **Suggest alternative** — different dates/room; the guest can accept or not.
5. **Unavailable** — those dates are closed. The guest cannot pay.

## If the server says no

The screen must not look “confirmed” only on that laptop. Sign-in as partner is required.

## What they must not do

Mark a stay **paid**. That is never a partner or admin button. Paid is a payment-provider / webhook job.
