# Contact / talk to an expert

**Who:** a guest who wants a human, not a checkout.

## What they do

1. Open `/contact`.
2. Leave name, phone, email, what they need, and WhatsApp consent.
3. Submit. They get a thank-you — not “an expert is calling you in 30 seconds” unless that is actually true.
4. Healingram staff (admin) can open that lead by id. The guest cannot.

Phone and email stay **out of the URL**.

## V1 limits

There is get-by-id, not a full inbox UI. Duplicate submits can create duplicate leads. WhatsApp is a handoff (`wa.me`), not the Business API.
